# Tools Over Frameworks

We often treat frameworks like religions. We pick one, learn its quirks, accept its opinions, and build our entire workflow around its constraints. But as you build more projects, you realize that frameworks come and go, while good tools stick around.

## The Problem with Heavy Frameworks

Frameworks love to take control. They dictate your folder structure, how you handle state, how you route, and how you build. Before you know it, you are spending more time fighting the framework's abstractions than solving your actual problem.

When you are trying to ship fast—whether it is a quick MVP or a dedicated API client—heavy boilerplate gets in the way.

## Why Tools Win

A tool does one job well and gets out of your way. It doesn't care how you structure your app or what state management library you use.

- **Flexibility:** You can swap a tool out tomorrow if something better comes along.
- **Clarity:** You understand how it works because it doesn't hide behind layers of magic.
- **Longevity:** Simple, well-built utilities outlive the trendiest JavaScript frameworks of the year.

Focus on mastering the right tools, and let your framework be whatever helps you ship today.
